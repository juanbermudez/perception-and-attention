# Accuracy audit: Touch topic and touch region guides

Scope: `pathways.ts` topic `touch` (intro, insight, caveat, steps 1–8). `region-guides.ts`: `medulla`, `vpl`, `s1`, `dorsalHorn`, `s2`, `postInsula`, plus `roles.touch` of `insula`, `cingulate`, `parietal`. Model checks used `region-anatomy.ts`, `regions.ts`, `src/data/atlas-data.json`, `src/data/functional-areas.json` and `research/atlas-meshes.txt`.

All 33 cited DOIs resolve on Crossref, and each title matches its citation. Abstracts come from Europe PMC. Full text was read for Willis & Westlund 1997, Dubin & Patapoutian 2010, Delhaye et al. 2018, Marshall et al. 2019 and Lamp et al. 2019. One extra source was found and checked: Afif et al. 2008, Pain 138:546–555, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pain.2008.02.004.

## 2. Counts

- About 175 factual statements checked: 85 in the topic and 90 in the region guides and roles in scope.
- 154 were OK. 21 were non-OK, grouped into 18 rows above:
  - WRONG: 2
  - OVERSTATED: 5
  - UNCLEAR: 11 statements in 10 rows
  - STYLE: 3 statements in 1 row
  - UNSUPPORTED: 0

Key checks that came back OK:
- **Marshall 2019.** The 19 patients, the C1/C2 lesion opposite the pain, loss of itch and temperature, and unchanged detection thresholds are all confirmed.
- **Mazzola 2012.** 4,160 stimulations in 164 patients, 1.4% evoking pain, and no pain from S1 or cingulate are all confirmed, so step 7's fact is correctly limited to that study.
- **Brooks 2005.** The hand coordinate (−40, −19, 14) matches the mean of single-subject peaks.
- **Lamp 2019.** The coordinate (−54, −26, 20) is the left OP1 peak. It comes from the right-hand ROI-study analysis and its conjunction with whole-brain studies; the main left-hemisphere whole-brain peak is (−48, −20, 20). This is acceptable, but the basis field could say "ROI studies".
- **Other single studies.** Pons 1987 (lesions in both directions), Lockwood 2013 (TMS at 120 ms; intensity affected, location not), Rainville 1997, Craig 2000 (right anterior insula for felt intensity), Graziano & Jones 2004 (VMpo is part of VPM), Dubin 2010 (C fibres 0.4–1.4 m/s; laminae I, II and V) and Delhaye 2018 (3a/3b/1/2 response types, larger receptive fields, curvature tuning in area 2, all four S1 areas projecting to S2) are all confirmed.
- **Model.** The dorsal horn anchor (0.65, −2.62, −0.12) is at the lower, dorsal end of the right medulla. The meshes named in each `limit` exist, and none of the insular gyri, parietal operculum, dorsal column nuclei or VPL are segmented. The S2 and posterior insula markers match `functional-areas.json`.

## 3. Could not verify

- **Aβ conduction speed of 35–75 m/s (step 1).** The listed sources are the Johnson 2001 abstract, which has no velocities, and Purves on NCBI Bookshelf, which is behind a reCAPTCHA that I did not bypass. The figure matches standard textbook tables (Kandel), so I left it OK.
- **Ploner 1999 lesion extent.** Only the abstract is available ("right-sided postcentral stroke"). See #8.
- **Olausson 2002 "slow stroking".** The abstract says only that C-tactile afferents were activated. I could not confirm the stroking speed without the full text. Low risk.
- **Penfield & Boldrey 1937.** No abstract. The step 3 fact matches the standard historical account.
- **StatPearls (NBK555915) and Macaluso (NBK92871).** Both are on Bookshelf (blocked), so only their Europe PMC abstracts were checked. The claims they support (the S1 map; parietal cortex combining touch and vision) are standard, and Delhaye 2018 supports them too.
- **Outside scope, but worth a look.** Step 8 and the cingulate region are labelled "anterior cingulate cortex", but the highlight is Destrieux's middle-anterior cingulate, which is anterior midcingulate cortex in Vogt's terms. Much pain-related "ACC" activity lies there, so this is a naming issue rather than an error. The `cingulate.limit` should say so.
