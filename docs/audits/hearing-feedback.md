Audited 2026-09-28 against `~/Desktop/basics-on-attention/src/content/` (pathways.ts, region-guides.ts, sources.ts, regions.ts, region-anatomy.ts) and `src/data/functional-areas.json`. No project files were edited.

Method: every DOI in scope, plus every new source proposed below, was resolved on Crossref, and all matched title, first author, journal and year. Full text was read where it was open: Chevillet 2011, DeWitt & Rauschecker 2012, Romanski 1999, Rauschecker & Scott 2009, Lopez-Poveda 2018, Lauer 2022, Delano 2007, Moerel 2014, Crandall 2015, Dimwamwa 2024, Mo 2024, Sherman & Usrey 2024, Fischl & Dale 2000, Nakajima 2019, Theyel 2010, Blot 2021, Brughera 2013 and Golshani 2001.
