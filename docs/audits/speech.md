# Accuracy audit: Speech and language

Scope: `speech` topic in `src/content/pathways.ts` (intro, insight, caveat, 5 steps, `sourceIds`); topic sources in `src/content/sources.ts`; region guides `temporal`, `spt`, `frontal`, `motor`, `meaning`; the `speech` role of `a1` (`a1R` has no `speech` role; its lateralization sentence is checked because it bears on the left/right claims); `label`/`where` in `src/content/regions.ts`; model placement in `scripts/prepare-atlas.mjs` and `src/content/region-anatomy.ts`. Fact-check log entries 36–42 were used as leads.

Method: every cited DOI was resolved on Crossref, and abstracts were read on Europe PMC. Open full text was read for Hamilton 2021, Flinker 2015, Bouchard 2013, Mesgarani 2014, Mesgarani & Chang 2012, Khanna 2024, Huth 2016, Silva 2022 and Hickok 2012. Additional evidence was resolved the same way (Gajardo-Vidal 2021 *Brain*; Fedorenko & Blank 2020 *TICS*; Fedorenko, Duncan & Kanwisher 2012 *Curr Biol*; Knecht 2000 *Brain*; Rice, Lambon Ralph & Hoffman 2015 *Cereb Cortex*; Hickok, Houde & Rong 2011 *Neuron*; Houde & Jordan 1998 *Science*; Stuart et al. 2002 *JASA*; Buchsbaum et al. 2011 *Brain Lang*; Leonard et al. 2024 *Nature*; Nourski et al. 2014 *NeuroImage*; Hickok, Okada & Serences 2009 *J Neurophysiol*; Zatorre, Belin & Penhune 2002 *TICS*; McGettigan & Scott 2012 *TICS*).

## 4. Notes on the fact-check log (entries 36–42)

- #37 says "Removing primary auditory cortex (HG) did not affect speech perception." It was one patient treated with thermocoagulation; this wording carried into step 1 (#6).
- #38 leaves out Flinker's timing (peak about 340 ms after the heard word, activity over before speech at about 1,200 ms), and that omission produced the "just before speaking" error (#10). The log correctly records the debate, but neither the log nor the content cites the main lesion evidence (Gajardo-Vidal 2021, *Brain*).
- #40 is correct that Silva 2022 is a review ("In this review") and that Castellucci 2022 comes from the Long lab. It gives no location for the mPrCG, which led to "just behind it" (#11).
- #42 already reconciles bilateral meaning with left-lateralized language, but the content leaves out the lesion-based disagreement that Huth 2016 itself raises (#3, #26).
