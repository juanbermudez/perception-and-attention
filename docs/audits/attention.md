## 3. Not verified, and why

- **Vogt 2005 full text** (only the abstract was available). The statement in row 27 that the highlighted "middle-anterior" cingulate corresponds to anterior midcingulate cortex is moderate confidence.
- **Auditory input to the SC from the inferior colliculus** (`sc.connections`): standard neuroanatomy, but not stated in Krauzlis 2013 or any listed source. Treated as OK. A source should be added if one is wanted.
- **`cingulate.connections`, thalamic pain input**: the listed source (Shyu & Vogt 2009) is largely rodent physiology. The primate midline, mediodorsal and intralaminar → ACC projection is standard but was not verified from a listed source.
- **Bruce & Goldberg 1985 part I** (definition of visual, movement and visuomovement FEF cells) was not fetched. The classification is confirmed indirectly by the Bruce 1985 part II abstract and Gregoriou 2012.
- **SC and LC marker geometry** was checked only against atlas bounding boxes and anchor coordinates, not against the rendered mesh surface. The "just above the inferior colliculus" placement is plausible but not measured.
- **Stanton 1988 abstract** is truncated by Europe PMC. The "largest terminal fields … in the ipsilateral superior colliculus" sentence was confirmed.
